## Assumptions

- Mechanism: R01.
- Institute: NHGRI. NHGRI expects human genomic data from its funded
  research to go to AnVIL, with the study registered in dbGaP. For another
  IC, dbGaP alone or an IC-specific repository may apply.
- Human participants: yes, with consent that permits controlled-access
  sharing through NIH repositories.
- Element 7: the WGS component is treated as large-scale human genomic data,
  so the GDS Policy applies and 7a and 7b are marked Yes.
- Volumes (~8 TB raw, ~80 GB processed) are illustrative and only affect the
  budget language.
